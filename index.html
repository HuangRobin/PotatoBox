PotatoBox

PotatoBox是一款纸带音乐盒与作曲工具，把机械音乐盒的趣味带到 iPhone 和 iPad。推入把手，向上拨动后松手，伴随旋律欣赏打孔纸带缓缓移动。

浏览内置古典与传统曲目，在纸带上作曲，导入支持的 MIDI 文件，并继续在后台聆听。

A paper-strip music box and composition tool

PotatoBox brings the joy of a mechanical music box to iPhone and iPad. Push in the handle, flick it upward and release it, then watch the punched paper strip move with the melody.

Explore built-in classical and traditional pieces, compose on the paper strip, import supported MIDI files, and listen in the background.
