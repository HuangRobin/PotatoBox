隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日 · 适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 上的 PotatoBox

本政策说明 PotatoBox 开发者如何处理与本应用相关的信息。

应用不收集个人数据

PotatoBox 无需注册或登录。应用不向开发者发送个人信息、使用记录或音乐作品，不包含广告、用户追踪或第三方分析服务。开发者不会通过应用收集、出售或共享你的个人数据。

设备上的文件与设置

你创作的音符、作品标题、速度、保存的作品和草稿保存在设备上的应用文件夹中，仅用于编辑、保存和播放。首次使用提示等设置保存在设备上。

导入 MIDI 时，应用通过系统文件选择器访问你主动选择的文件，并在设备上读取和处理音乐数据，不将文件上传给开发者。文件可能来自 iCloud Drive 或其他文件服务，这些服务按照你自己的设置和相应服务的政策运行。

保留、删除与备份

作品保留在设备上，直到你在应用中删除相应作品或通过系统删除应用数据。删除 App 会移除其本地应用数据；系统备份或你另存的文件可能仍然存在，需要按相应系统或服务的方式管理。设备备份是否包含应用数据取决于你的 Apple 系统设置；PotatoBox 不运营云端同步或备份服务。

系统功能与外部服务

后台音频和系统播放控件用于播放音乐。应用不录制麦克风，也不访问联系人或位置。你主动打开应用中的外部网站或 App Store 链接时，相关服务按其自身隐私政策处理信息。Apple 的系统诊断与分析设置由你和 Apple 管理。

技术支持

如果你通过 GitHub Issues 提交反馈，开发者会查看你主动提供的账号公开信息、问题内容和附件，用于回复、排查和改进应用。Issues 为公开页面，请不要提交私人信息或不希望公开的文件。GitHub 按其 隐私声明 处理网站访问和账号数据。你可以编辑自己的反馈，或通过 Issues 请求开发者协助处理与反馈相关的信息。

儿童隐私

PotatoBox 不通过应用收集任何年龄用户的个人数据，包括儿童的个人数据。

政策更新与联系

应用的数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本页及生效日期。如有隐私问题，请通过 技术支持页面 联系开发者。

Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026 · Applies to PotatoBox for iPhone and iPad

This policy explains how the PotatoBox developer handles information related to the app.

No personal data collection by the app

PotatoBox requires no registration or sign-in. The app does not send personal information, usage history or musical works to the developer. It contains no advertising, user tracking or third-party analytics services. The developer does not collect, sell or share your personal data through the app.

Files and settings on your device

Notes, work titles, tempo, saved compositions and drafts are stored in the app’s folder on your device for editing, saving and playback. Settings such as first-use tips are also stored locally.

When you import MIDI, the app accesses the file you choose through the system file picker and processes its musical data on your device. Files are not uploaded to the developer. Files may come from iCloud Drive or other file providers, which operate under your settings and their own policies.

Retention, deletion and backups

Works remain on your device until you delete them in the app or remove app data through the system. Deleting the app removes its local app data; system backups or copies you saved elsewhere may remain and must be managed through the relevant system or service. Inclusion in device backups depends on your Apple system settings. PotatoBox operates no cloud sync or backup service.

System features and external services

Background audio and system playback controls are used to play music. The app does not record your microphone or access contacts or location. External websites or App Store links you choose to open handle information under their own privacy policies. Apple system diagnostics and analytics are controlled by your settings and Apple.

Support requests

If you submit feedback through GitHub Issues, the developer reviews the public account information, report and attachments you choose to provide to respond, troubleshoot and improve the app. Issues are public: do not post private information or files you do not want published. GitHub handles website and account data under its Privacy Statement. You can edit your own report or request the developer’s assistance with information related to your feedback through Issues.

Children’s privacy

PotatoBox does not collect personal data through the app from users of any age, including children.

Updates and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page.
